⏺ I didn't change any files. Below are 20 issues with SPEC.md, GLOSSARY.md and
  schemas.ts, grouped by how sure I am. The ones marked ⚠️ are the ones I'm not
  sure about. I checked every example in the glossary and README against the
  fixtures and they all match (the JPM FWP, the AAPL Form 4 and DEF 14A, the 8-K
  accession number, SPOT's 8 20-Fs and 97 6-Ks).

  Likely mistakes

  1. The fixtures were probably downloaded on 2026-11-14, not 2026-11-16, and 
  the spec never records the date.
  - The tests depend on this fixed "today", so it should be written down.
  - The newest acceptanceDateTime in all three files is JPM
    2026-11-14T02:03:23Z, a Friday evening in New York. AAPL's newest is 11-12
    and SPOT's is 11-13.
  - Yet JPM has 10+ 424B2/FWP rows with filingDate: "2026-11-16", all accepted
    on 11-13 or 11-14. The SEC gave them Monday's date in advance.
  - So a filing's date can be later than the download day. The summary window.to
    = today has no rule for filings dated after to. Are they counted?
  - ⚠️ I can't prove the download day. It could also have been early on 11-16
    before any new filings came in.

  2. Sort tie-breaks have no direction.
  - "then acceptanceDateTime, then accessionNumber" doesn't say ascending or
    descending, or whether they flip when sort=asc.
  - Ties do happen. AAPL rows 3 and 4 are two Form 4s, both filed 2026-11-03 and
    accepted at 21:15:17Z, with accessions …-041803 and …-041799. JPM has 61
    such pairs, and SPOT has 1.
  - The SEC's own order puts the higher accession number first. That makes a
    sensible default for desc.

  3. url is a path, not a full link.
  - /Archives/edgar/data/... has no https://www.sec.gov in front of it.
  - A frontend <a href> would point at the app's own server.

  4. Filings that were never electronic get links that probably don't work.
  - For example, AAPL NO ACT 2016-12-05 has primaryDocument
    9999999997-17-000002.paper and description "AUTO-GENERATED PAPER DOCUMENT".
  - AAPL and SPOT have 22 .paper/.txt/.pdf primary documents each, and JPM
    has 2.
  - ⚠️ I haven't checked what the SEC actually serves at those URLs.

  5. The "empty string → null" rule is too broad for the schema.
  - The spec says "If any values return "" we map it to null". But only
    reportDate and description are nullable in Filing.
  - In the fixtures, only those two fields are ever "" among the fields we
    return. For example, 95 empty primaryDocDescription in AAPL, and JPM
    reportDate empty in 26,284 of its rows.
  - The rule should name the two fields.

  6. The 422 error body doesn't match the other errors.
  - 404 and 502 return ApiError {code, message}. 422 returns Elysia's own
    validation shape, which isn't in schemas.ts.
  - The frontend will have to handle two error shapes.

  7. GLOSSARY.md has a heading that doesn't match the data.
  - The row is titled filings.file, but the JSON key is filings.files.

  8. A GLOSSARY.md rule doesn't hold for every filing.
  - The acceptanceDateTime row says "late-day submissions get the next business
    day's filingDate".
  - AAPL Form 4 0000320193-23-000093 was accepted 2023-10-04T01:09:17Z (21:09
    New York time) and still has filingDate: "2023-10-03".
  - ⚠️ This is likely because Forms 3/4/5 have a later cutoff, but I haven't
    confirmed that.

  Unclear points (the spec needs to decide)

  9. The ticker lookup can't be tested properly with these fixtures.
  - company_tickers.json lists BRK-B, NVDA and MSFT, but there are no
    submissions files for them. So the BRK.B = BRK-B rule can't be tested from
    start to finish.
  - JPM's submissions list 9 tickers (JPM-PC, AMJB, VYLD, …), but
    company_tickers.json only has JPM. Is JPM-PC a 404?
  - ⚠️ I don't know whether the real company_tickers.json lists those extra
    tickers.

  10. company.ticker isn't defined.
  - It could be the ticker as the user typed it, the normalized form, or the
    entry from company_tickers. For JPM, which of its 9 tickers?

  11. The order of input clean-up for tickers is unclear.
  - Does duplicate removal happen before or after the ./- normalization? That
    decides whether BRK.B,BRK-B counts as one ticker.
  - Is the 20-ticker limit counted before or after duplicates are removed?
  - Are tickers=, and tickers=AAPL,,MSFT "empty" (422), or are the empty entries
    just dropped?
  - Two different tickers for the same company (JPM,JPM-PC) would give two
    results for one CIK.

  12. Old and new form names are split.
  - AAPL and SPOT both have SC 13G/SC 13G/A and SCHEDULE 13G/SCHEDULE 13G/A.
  - With exact matching, form=SC 13G misses the newer filings, and countsByForm
    splits them into separate counts.
  - That follows the spec as written, but you should confirm it's what you want.

  13. Amendment rules for latestAnnualReport aren't stated.
  - Only latest10K says it ignores amendments. Does latestAnnualReport ignore
    10-K/A, 20-F/A and 40-F/A?
  - Are other annual forms like 10-KT included?
  - There's no fixture with an amended annual report or a 40-F, so none of this
    can be tested.

  14. A null latest10K/latestAnnualReport can mean two things.
  - It could mean the company never filed one, or that the filing is older than
    what recent holds.
  - JPM's recent covers only 2025-11-14 → 2026-11-16, with exactly one 10-K
    (2026-02-13). Its earlier 10-Ks are in the 72 files the API doesn't load.
  - CompanySummary has no coverage/complete flag, unlike FilingsPage.

  15. The 12-month window has fencepost problems.
  - filingDate >= today − 365 with today included covers 366 calendar days.
  - That also isn't "12 months" in a leap year, and the spec says "12-month
    counts" in one place and "365 days" in another.

  16. coverage has no rule for an empty recent.
  - from and to are required strings, so they can't be null.
  - ⚠️ No fixture has an empty recent, so this might never happen.

  17. Failures for the whole summary request aren't defined.
  - If company_tickers.json can't be fetched, is the response a 502, or a 200
    with every ticker in errors[] as UPSTREAM_UNAVAILABLE?
  - The spec's "even if every ticker fails" suggests the 200, but it doesn't say
    so.

  18. The edge cases for form aren't covered.
  - What do form=10-K, and form=, do: empty filter or 422?
  - Do repeated spaces like DEF  14A count as a match?

  Lower priority / unsure

  19. ⚠️ recent may not always cover the 365-day window.
  - For JPM it just about covers it: the oldest row is 2025-11-14, and
    files[0].filingTo is 2025-11-12.
  - The SEC says recent holds at least a year of filings, so this is probably
    safe. Worth a note in the spec.

  20. ⚠️ The README's guess that SPOT lost foreign-issuer status looks unlikely.
  - SPOT kept filing 6-Ks after 2026-03 (12 of them), next to the new Forms 3/4.
    It hasn't filed any 10-Q or 10-K.

  The ones I'd fix first are 1, 2 and 3 (the test date, sort tie-breaks and full
  URLs), then 9 through 11 (ticker handling).
